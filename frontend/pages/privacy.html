Privacy Policy

Your privacy and data sovereignty are paramount in the realm of Life Quest. Learn what information we collect and how we safeguard it.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Life Quest ("we", "us", "our") collects, protects, uses, and discloses information when you use our web application located at lifequest-pzxa.onrender.com.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information necessary to provide, calculate, and synchronize your gameplay progression:

  • Account Information: When you register an account, we collect your username, email address, and a salted cryptographic hash of your password (generated via bcrypt with 10 salt rounds). We never store plaintext passwords.
  • Gameplay & Progression Data: Quests created and completed, category selections (e.g. Coding, Fitness, Study), earned Experience Points (XP), in-game Gold balances, streak counts, unlocked achievements, and attribute distribution (Strength, Intellect, Discipline, Vitality, Focus, Social).
  • Avatar Media: If you use our Avatar Studio to upload a custom profile picture, the cropped image data is stored securely in your user record and served to display your character across devices and leaderboards.
  • Authentication Logs: Timestamps of recent logins and session identifiers stored in our persistent session store.

3. Third-Party Authentication (Google OAuth)

If you choose to sign in via Google OAuth, we receive your Google user ID, name, email address, and profile picture URL as permitted by your Google privacy settings. We do not obtain access to your Google password, Google Drive, or any other personal Google account data.

4. How We Use Your Information

We utilize your data strictly for legitimate platform purposes:

  • Authenticating your identity and maintaining secure sessions via encrypted cookies.
  • Calculating non-linear RPG level-ups, attribute distribution, and streak milestones on our server.
  • Displaying public leaderboards on the Hall of Champions (only displaying your username, country flag, level, character class, and avatar).
  • Preventing cheating, abusive behavior, and duplicate task exploits.

5. Data Security & Storage

We implement robust technical and architectural security measures to safeguard your information:

  • Encrypted Transport: All data transmitted between your browser and our server is secured using Transport Layer Security (HTTPS/SSL).
  • Encrypted Database: Application data is stored in a secure cloud MySQL/TiDB database with strict TLS transport and connection-pooling isolation.
  • HttpOnly Cookies: Authentication tokens are stored inside browser cookies flagged with httpOnly and SameSite=Lax to prevent Cross-Site Scripting (XSS) extraction.
  • Tenant Isolation: Backend API routes verify account ownership on every database operation; users cannot view, edit, or delete another user's personal quests or attributes.

6. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Life Quest uses strictly necessary first-party session cookies to keep you signed in. We do not use third-party advertising cookies, behavioral tracking pixels, or sell your personal data to data brokers.

7. Your Rights & Data Sovereignty

You have complete ownership and control over your personal data:

  • Access & Correction: You can review and update your profile information, password, and custom avatar at any time in the Settings page.
  • Data Deletion: You have the right to request deletion of your account and all associated gameplay logs, quests, and statistics.

8. Children's Privacy

Life Quest does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can promptly delete the data.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us via our public repository: https://github.com/overclockedhackathon/LifeQuest.
